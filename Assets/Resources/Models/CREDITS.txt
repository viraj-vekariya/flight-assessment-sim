Source: https://kenney.nl  — License: CC0 1.0 (no attribution required; recorded
for the thesis). OBJ format; city/house models use the shared Textures/colormap.png
atlas, nature models use solid material colours.

Loaded at runtime by ModelLibrary.cs (Resources.Load) and placed on the terrain by
WorldBuilder. If the models are absent/unimported, WorldBuilder FALLS BACK to its
code-built primitive buildings/trees, so the world always builds.
